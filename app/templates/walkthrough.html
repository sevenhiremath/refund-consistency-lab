About this data

Provider notifications are simulated, and the provider lookup is simulated too. No merchant payment is processed and no live provider is contacted.

A lookup is not part of normal duplicate handling. Duplicate deliveries are settled by the event id alone. The lookup exists to supply information that a dropped notification never carried.
