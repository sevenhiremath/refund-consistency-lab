Refund notification processing

A refund record you can defend when the notifications disagree.

Refund Consistency maintains refund records from provider notifications, so duplicates, late arrivals and conflicting evidence resolve to one answer you can explain.

Try the demo Integrate

What it does and does not do

Maintains One record per refund, from the notifications you deliver, with the evidence behind every decision.
Does not move money No payment execution, no outgoing refunds, no provider credentials. It records and classifies; the payment system still moves the funds.
Shows its work Confirmed amounts, amounts under review, and the reason a refund was not settled.

How a notification becomes a record

A processor that keys on the provider event id handles duplicate deliveries without asking anyone. The same event id is applied once, however many times it arrives. That is what this service does.

The demo also includes a deliberately naive baseline that counts every notification instead. It is there to show the failure, not to describe the product. A lookup exists for a different problem: a notification that never arrived cannot be recovered by rereading what you have.

Where the demo fits

The simulator is the public demonstration of the service, not a second product. Every figure in it is produced by the same processing rules the API exposes. Provider notifications and provider lookups in the demo are simulated.

No real merchant payments are processed here. There are no customers, no accounts and no live provider connections.
